# Privacy Policy - FoxyCookieManager

**Effective Date:** May 11, 2024  
**Last Updated:** May 11, 2024

## Introduction

FoxyCookieManager ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our Chrome extension.

## Data Collection

**We DO NOT collect any personal data.**

FoxyCookieManager:
- Does NOT collect any personal information
- Does NOT track your browsing activity
- Does NOT send any data to external servers
- Does NOT share your information with third parties
- Does NOT use analytics or tracking tools

## Data Storage

### Local Storage Only

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API:

- **Cookies**: Stored locally in your browser
- **Saved Cookie List**: Stored locally (up to 50 cookies)
- **Theme Preference**: Stored locally
- **Extension Settings**: Stored locally

### What We Store

The extension stores:
1. Cookie data for Facebook, Instagram, and Meta (only when you save them)
2. Your theme preference (light/dark/system)
3. Custom names you assign to saved cookies

All stored data remains on your device and is never transmitted externally.

## Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions:

### cookies
- **Purpose**: To read and write cookies for Facebook, Instagram, and Meta
- **Usage**: Only when you actively use the extension features
- **Scope**: Limited to *.facebook.com, *.instagram.com, *.meta.com

### storage
- **Purpose**: To save your cookie list and preferences locally
- **Usage**: To store up to 50 cookies on your device
- **Scope**: Local storage only, no cloud sync

### activeTab
- **Purpose**: To access the current tab when you use the extension
- **Usage**: To determine which platform (Facebook/Instagram/Meta) you're on
- **Scope**: Only the active tab, only when extension is opened

## Data Security

- All data is stored locally using Chrome's secure storage API
- No data is transmitted over the network
- No external servers or databases are used
- You have full control over your data

## Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

1. **Delete individual cookies**: Use the "Delete" button in the Manager tab
2. **Clear all data**: Uninstall the extension
3. **Clear browser data**: Use Chrome's "Clear browsing data" feature

## Third-Party Services

FoxyCookieManager does NOT use any third-party services, including:
- No analytics (Google Analytics, etc.)
- No crash reporting
- No advertising networks
- No external APIs

## Cookies

The extension manages cookies for:
- Facebook (*.facebook.com)
- Instagram (*.instagram.com)
- Meta (*.meta.com)

These cookies are only accessed when you use the extension and are stored locally on your device.

## User Responsibility

**Important Security Notice:**

- Cookies can provide access to your accounts
- We are NOT responsible if you voluntarily share your cookies with third parties
